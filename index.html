HOYA WORDLE

How to Play

Guess the GULC WORDLE in 6 tries. Every answer is a Georgetown Law — or law school in general — term.

Each guess must be a real 5-letter word. Type it in and hit enter; if it's not in the dictionary, you'll be told to try again.

After each guess, the color of the tiles will show how close your guess was.
